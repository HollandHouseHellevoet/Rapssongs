CHASSIS · SHARED HEADER + FOOTER

The chassis.
One header. One footer. Twenty‑three sites.

Everything the reader sees on rojasreport.com, waysandmeans.rojasreport.com, poh.rojasreport.com, academic.rojasreport.com, and the 19 that follow — the shell is the same. Only the subdomain badge, the context rail, and the sister‑site highlight change. This page documents the pattern.

Header · three zones

1 · BRAND BAR
Brand mark + wordmark + // SUBDOMAIN badge on the left, primary nav center, ⌘K entity lookup on the right. On the hub the subdomain badge reads CONSOLE; on each subdomain it reads the desk name in amber mono. Sticky, 88% opacity with backdrop blur.
2 · CONTEXT RAIL
Second row under the brand bar. Two modes: QUESTIONS strip on the hub (rotating four-persona queries), BREADCRUMB strip on desk & entity pages (CONSOLE / WAYS & MEANS / HR 4183). Same height either way so the layout stays consistent.
3 · OPTIONAL SUB‑NAV
On desk homepages, a third rail appears with desk‑local sections (OVERVIEW · ENTITIES · DOSSIERS · FILINGS · METHOD). Hidden on the hub.

Footer · three zones

1 · SISTER SITES STRIP
All 23 desks rendered as amber-mono pills. The current site (e.g., WAYSANDMEANS) is filled amber; live desks are outlined; roadmap desks are dimmed with · SOON. This is how a reader on the Ways & Means desk finds their way to POH, or from POH to Academic.
2 · FOUR COLUMN GRID
THE CONSOLE (about · method · press · contact) · FOR READERS (the four personas) · REGISTRIES (top six desks · all 23 →) · METHOD & LEGAL (methodology · corrections · attribution · terms · privacy). First column doubles as the motto & manifesto slot.
3 · FOOT META
Small RR mark + ROJASREPORT // STANDING INTELLIGENCE on the left. Version tag + copyright + legal micro-links on the right.

Everything above is implemented as a single <ConsoleShell> component in the eventual Next.js scaffold, parameterized by { subdomain, crumbs, subnav, currentDeskSlug }. Twenty-three sites share one codebase; one design lock = twenty-three redesigns done.
